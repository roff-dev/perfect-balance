Privacy

This site is made and run by me, Kieron Oates. I don't use cookies, I don't have accounts, and your progress stays in your browser.

What's kept on your device

Your results, your streak, your Freeplay settings and whether you've eaten the cookie and seen the opening on the start screen are saved in your browser's local storage, under the name pb:v1. They don't leave your device unless you copy a backup code and take it somewhere yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Counting visits

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people play. It doesn't use cookies or anything stored on your device, and it doesn't identify you or follow you between sites. It records the page you visited, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, and your country.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to send you the pages, and may keep it briefly in its logs for security. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that.

Sharing your result

Share hands the text or the result card to your device's share sheet or clipboard. Nothing is sent to me.

Your rights

I don't collect anything that identifies you, so there's nothing about you for me to show, correct or delete. If you have a question about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
