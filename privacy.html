Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This site is made and run by me, Kieron Oates. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies, and I don't sell or share anything about you.

What stays on your device

A few things are kept in your browser's local storage, under the name pb:v1, so the game remembers them between visits:

  • your rounds for each daily and archive puzzle, including one you've left to finish later
  • your streak
  • your Freeplay settings
  • whether you've eaten the cookie and seen the opening on the start screen

None of it leaves your device unless you copy a backup code and take it somewhere yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Sharing your result

Share hands the result text to your device's share sheet or clipboard. Nothing is sent to me.

Who your browser talks to

Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any host, it handles your IP address to send you the pages and may keep it briefly in its logs for security. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: it sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and doesn't follow you between sites. It records the page you visited, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, and your country. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

There's no sign-up and nothing to give, so the site doesn't ask for your age.

Your rights and questions

I don't collect anything that identifies you, so there's nothing about you for me to show, correct or delete. If you have a question about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.

If I change this page, the date at the top changes too.
